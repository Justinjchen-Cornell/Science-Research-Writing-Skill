# 第 2 章笔记：How to Write an Introduction

## 四部件模型（漏斗：宽→窄）
① 建立重要性 + 背景（1-3句）
   - 句式: [Topic] has received much attention in recent years due to...
   - 词汇: plays a key role / of growing interest / well-documented
   - 时态: Present Simple / Present Perfect
② 文献综述（2-4句，必须有序）
   - 三种排序: 时间 / 按方法分组 / 由泛到专
   - 句式: [Author] et al. showed that... and more recently, [Author2] established...
   - 时态: Past Simple / Present Perfect
③ 研究空白（1-2句，最重要的转折）
   - 转折词开头: However / Although / Nevertheless
   - 句式: However, little attention has been paid to... / Few studies have focused on...
   - 时态: Present Perfect（空白还在）
   - 不要用问句
④ 描述本文（2-3句）
   - 句式: The present paper presents... / This study focuses on...
   - 目的句: The aim of this study was...（Past Simple）
   - 哑主语: This paper / The present paper

## 时态对
- Present Simple = 永久/事实；Present Continuous = 临时
- **语气开关**: 结果用 Present Simple 陈述 = 宣布它为普遍规律；Past Simple = 只属于本研究
- Past Simple = 过去的事与现在无关；Present Perfect = 与现在相关（空白还在）
- 灵魂对比: "little attention WAS paid"（可能已解决）vs "HAS been paid"（至今没人做）
- **时态变化 = 功能变化，绝不随机换**

## 连接词 5 组
- 原因: due to / because / since / as
- 结果: therefore / as a result / consequently / hence
- 对比: however / whereas / while / by contrast
- 出乎意料: although / despite / nevertheless / nonetheless
- 补充: in addition / moreover / furthermore
- 坑: since 双义；so 太口语；conversely=完全相反

## 句间连接 4 法
1. 重叠（重复概念） 2. 代称（it/this method） 3. 分号/关系从句 4. 连接词

## we / 被动 / 分段
- we = 研究团队；人类用 It is known that...
- 哑主语优于 agentless passive
- 一段 = 主题句 + 支撑句；单句段/超长段都是错误

## 从目标论文偷到的句式库
- "X has revolutionized [领域]"（Logic-Q ①）
- "previous works present that X are prone to [问题]"（②）
- "However, even state-of-the-art X struggle to [空白]"（③）
- "we propose a [特征] framework, called [名字]"（④）
- "falls short of / likewise / unevenly addressed"（survey 空白高级词）
