# 第 4 章笔记：Writing about Results

## 核心: Results do not speak for themselves
- 不评论 = 放弃解读权 = 读者可能得出相反结论
- 23% 例子: "as many as 23%"（显强）vs "only 23%"（显弱）vs "23%"（浪费）

## 四部件菜单
① 回顾目的/已有研究/方法 + 总览
② 邀请查看（As can be seen in Fig.1）+ 关键结果 + 对比
③ 问题（提及+最小化）
④ 含义（hedged）

## 四套语言系统
1. Sequence 8组: beforehand→at the beginning→then(只表序)→soon(短)→later(长)→when(同时)→at the end→afterwards
2. Frequency 11组: always(100%)→generally→regularly→often→more often than not→**as often as not(50%)**→sometimes→occasionally→rarely→hardly ever→never
   - 主观性: 18% 相对预期也可以是 frequently
3. Quantity 5组: 显大(considerable/as many as/substantial) / 显小(barely/negligible/as few as) / 强调(even higher/by far) / 接近(almost/nearly) / 不表态(some/moderate)
4. Causality: 强(cause/produce/result in) → 中(contribute to/a factor in) → 弱(be linked to/related to)
   - 方向陷阱: x results FROM y = y是原因; x results IN y = y是结果

## Hedging 阶梯（5级，只升到证据允许）
L0 caused → L1 was linked to → L2 evidence suggests → L3 often caused → L4 may have caused
- 堆太多 = 句子没意义

## GOR Results 示范要点
- "in all four cases" = 100%频率词但n=4 → 必须立刻加"sample is limited"对冲
- 不写强因果: "was driven by a rally in crude oil in three of the four episodes"
- 含义句标配: suggest + may + although
