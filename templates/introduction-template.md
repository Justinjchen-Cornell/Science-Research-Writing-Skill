# Introduction Template
> Fill the placeholders. Keep the component ORDER — it is the model.
> For each slot, pick one sentence pattern (or adapt from your target articles).

## ① Establish importance + background (2-4 sentences)

**[Pattern A — significance]** [Topic] has received much attention in recent years due to [reason, e.g. its economic benefits].

**[Pattern B — fact]** [Topic] is [general fact about topic] and is produced/obtained by [process].

**[Pattern C — narrowing]** It has many possible uses in [field 1] and has also been investigated as [field 2]. However, it has been found to be [limitation of current state].

## ② Literature review (2-4 sentences, ordered chronologically / by approach / general→specific)

**[Pattern]** One way to [address the problem] is to [approach]. For example, [Author] et al. showed that [finding 1], and more recently, [Author2] established [finding 2].

## ③ Gap (1-2 sentences — signal with However/Although)

**[Pattern A]** However, although [previous finding], little attention has been paid to [your gap].

**[Pattern B]** Few studies have focused on [gap]. There remains a need for [what's missing].

## ④ Present paper (2-3 sentences — Present Simple for the paper, Past Simple for the aim)

**[Pattern]** The present paper presents [what you did]. On the basis of [criteria/approach], it then describes [methodology preview]. [This combination / the results] [announce your finding].

---
**Checklist**: [ ] Started with significance, not the problem  [ ] Tense change at the gap (Past Simple → Present Perfect)  [ ] Every sentence connected to the previous  [ ] Ended by describing the paper
