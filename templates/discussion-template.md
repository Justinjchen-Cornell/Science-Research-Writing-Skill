# Discussion Template
> The Introduction in reverse. End by opening the door for future work.

## ① Revisit previous research + your methodology/results (2-4 sentences)

**[Pattern]** Prior work has documented [finding] ([Author], year). However, these studies have either been [limitation]. In this study we tested [what you did]. We found that [key result].

## ② Map to existing research (1-2 sentences)

**[Pattern]** These findings extend those of [Author], confirming that [relationship]. The improvements noted in our study were unrelated to [variables].

## ③ Achievement + implications + applications (2-4 sentences — modal-calibrated!)

**[Pattern A — implication]** This study therefore indicates that [finding] may [address need] across [population/context].

**[Pattern B — novelty (only with "to our knowledge")]** Most notably, this is the first study to our knowledge to [claim].

**[Pattern C — application]** Our results provide [evidence] for [application] and suggest that [approach] appears to be effective in [purpose].

## ④ Limitations + future work (2-3 sentences — limitations are DOORS, not confessions)

**[Pattern]** However, some limitations are worth noting. Although [positive result], [limitation]. Future work should therefore include [specific follow-up] designed to [goal].

---
**Modal calibration (ch05)**: no modal = empirical fact | may/might/could = possible | should/ought to = probable OR advisable | must/have to = virtually certain (but unproven!) or necessary
**Never**: claim novelty without "to our knowledge"; state a limitation without a future direction.
