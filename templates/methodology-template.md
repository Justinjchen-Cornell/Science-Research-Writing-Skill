# Methodology Template
> Menu, not checklist: include only components that apply to your work.
> KEY RULE: Present Simple passive = standard procedure; Past Simple passive = YOUR steps.

## ① Overview + purpose + source + background (1-3 sentences)

**[Pattern]** The current investigation involved [what you did]. [Materials/equipment] were supplied by [source]. [Site/system] is located in [location] and has been frequently used to [background justification].

## ② Specific details + justification + care (the body — most of the section)

**[Pattern A — precise detail]** A total of [N] samples was collected in [container, size, material] which were filled to the top and then sealed tightly to prevent contamination.

**[Pattern B — standard vs your work]** [Standard: A flexible section is inserted in the pipe.] [Yours: The samples WERE collected using ...] <- the tense change is the only signal of your contribution!

**[Pattern C — justification]** This method was chosen because [reason].

**[Pattern D — care]** Care was taken to [precaution]. To minimise [risk], [action].

## ③ Relate to other studies (1-2 sentences)

**[Pattern]** Samples were prepared using a revised version of the [method] established by [Institute/Author]. The procedure followed [Author] (year), with [modification].

## ④ Problems (only if you had any — minimise, pair with a positive)

**[Pattern]** Although [N] samples were at risk of [problem], variation among samples was negligible. / [Problem] was minimised by [action].
