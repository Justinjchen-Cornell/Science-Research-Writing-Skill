# Results Template
> Menu. The golden rule: RESULTS DO NOT SPEAK FOR THEMSELVES — comment on every figure/number.

## ① Revisit aim/existing research + overview (1-3 sentences)

**[Pattern]** Data obtained in previous studies indicated that [baseline finding]. In our study, [method] was used to [aim].

## ② Invitation + key results + comparisons (the body)

**[Pattern A — invitation]** Figure [N] shows the results obtained using [method].

**[Pattern B — key result with comment]** As can be seen, [result] was significantly [higher/lower] than [comparison], which is consistent with results obtained in previous studies.

**[Pattern C — anomaly]** However, the data were not consistent with these results for [condition].

**[Pattern D — comparison]** The [finding] were in line with those of [Author] et al. / differed from [Author]'s findings.

## ③ Problems (1 sentence, minimised)

**[Pattern]** A quantitative analysis based on [method] revealed that [result] slightly exceeds the [limit]. Nevertheless, [positive takeaway].

## ④ Implications (1-2 sentences — hedged!)

**[Pattern]** These results suggest that [implication] may provide [benefit] for [application].

---
**Frequency/quantity language pick-list (ch04)**: high: considerable, substantial, in as many as N% | low: barely, negligible, in only N% | neutral: some, moderate
**Sequence (ch04)**: beforehand -> at the beginning -> then (order only) -> soon (short gap) -> later (long gap) -> when (simultaneous) -> at the end -> afterwards
